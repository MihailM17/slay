Rules of the island

Money

Every territory earns 1 gold per clear hex each turn, then pays wages: ♟2 · ♝6 · ♞18 · ♚54. Can't pay? Every man starves into a grave, then a pine. Castles cost nothing.

Buy X peasant (10g) or C castle (15g, defends 2). Only peasants are sold — grow armies by stacking two men (1+1=♝, 1+2=♞, max ♚).

War

Click a bright man, then a glowing target. Attacks must be strictly stronger than the defence. Men, ♜2, houses 1 and graves 1 also guard neighbouring own hexes.

♟ can't touch ♟ lines · ♝ kills ♟ · only ♞+ cracks a ♜.

Cutting

Split a foe in two and the half without the house starts at 0 gold and starves. Linking your lands merges treasuries. Gold houses (⌂) can buy; grey ones are broke.

Trees spread every turn and pay nothing — keep chopping.

Modes

Single vs the AI. Multiplayer passes one screen between 2–4 humans (AI fills empty seats). Tutorial teaches the first moves. Level creator paints custom islands with the Land/Water/Start tools — click a Start hex again to cycle P1→P4.
